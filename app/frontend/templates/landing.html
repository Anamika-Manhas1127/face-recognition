Under the Hood

Ultralytics YOLOv11 is a state-of-the-art object detection architecture that extracts and draws labels for dozens of everyday items in real time.

Multi-task Cascaded Convolutional Networks segment frames to extract coordinate boundaries of eyes, nose, and mouth, ensuring optimal detection.

Uses InceptionResnetV1 trained on VGGFace2 to map face geometry into a 512-dimensional vector. These vectors are cross-compared to identify registered names.
Priority Overlays

To maintain a clean and clutter-free screen overlay, the system automatically suppresses standard YOLO bounding boxes on humans. It focuses directly on face registration classification, preventing multiple overlapping boxes on the same body.
